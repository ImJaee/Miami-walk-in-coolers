Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about our walk-ins and how ordering works. Don’t see your question? Give us a call.

Do you ship nationwide?

Yes, we ship to all 50 states. Walk-ins ship as panels built for simple assembly on site.

How fast can you build my walk-in?

Fast. Lead times depend on size and options, so call us or send a quote request for a current timeline.

Do you install?

No. We’re a manufacturer, not an installation company. Our panels are built to go together easily, and most customers use their own contractor or local refrigeration tech.

Do you service or repair walk-ins?

No. We build and sell the equipment. For service, contact a local refrigeration company.

Can you help me figure out what size I need?

Yes. Tell us what you’re storing and how much space you have, and we’ll help you size the box and the refrigeration.

Do you do custom sizes?

Yes. We build standard sizes or custom to your exact measurements, with options like glass doors, sliding doors and windows.

Can you build just a door?

Yes. We build custom cooler and freezer doors to fit any opening, including replacements.

How do I get a quote?

Call (786) 592-1077 or fill out our quote form. Tell us the size, cooler or freezer, floor or no floor, door needs and your ship-to ZIP code.
