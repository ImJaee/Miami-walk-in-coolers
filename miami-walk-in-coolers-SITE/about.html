About us

Our story

The people who build your box

Miami Walk-In Coolers is a family business, now in its third generation, backed by over 40 years of industry experience and more than a decade specializing in walk-in coolers and freezers.

We manufacture every box ourselves, using materials made in the U.S.A., and ship to customers in every state. From large national chains to local family-run restaurants, we give every customer the same thing: quality, good prices and fast lead times.

When you call us, you’re talking to the people that build your box: straight answers and a fast quote.
