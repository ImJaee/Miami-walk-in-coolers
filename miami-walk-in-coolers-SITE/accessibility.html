Accessibility Statement

We're committed to a website everyone can use.

Miami Walk-In Coolers & Freezers is committed to making our website accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA and continually work to improve the experience for all visitors.

What we do

  • Use clear text, strong color contrast, and readable font sizes.
  • Provide descriptive alt text for images.
  • Support keyboard navigation and screen readers.
  • Design pages that adapt to phones, tablets, and desktops.

Run into a barrier?

If you have any trouble using this site, please let us know and we'll help right away — and fix the issue. Contact us at miamiwalkincoolers@gmail.com or (786) 592-1077. We welcome your feedback.
